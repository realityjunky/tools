#### 3.4. Agreement between Xpert and tNGS

Among 103 children, the concordant results were obtained for 80 children with 11 tNGS + Xpert + and 69 tNGS – Xpert –, and the results were weakly consistent (κ = 0.361). However, agreement between tNGS and Xpert was moderate among PTB patients (κ = 0.505), bronchoalveolar lavage fluid (κ = 0.617) and sputum and pus specimen (κ = 0.421) (Supplementary Table 2).
